# Code of Conduct

AI Meeting is a place for practical, respectful collaboration in English or Japanese.

## Our standards

We welcome clear questions, reproducible reports, thoughtful reviews, and contributions that respect users, maintainers, and upstream authors. Assume good intent, explain disagreements with concrete examples, and keep feedback focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted contact, sexualized behavior, and publishing private or confidential information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, meeting recordings, participant details, or private conversations.

## Applying these rules

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when these standards are not followed. They will explain the reason when it is safe to do so.

Report a security issue through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For conduct concerns, use the private business inquiry form linked from the repository's issue template and include only the context needed to review the concern.

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project spaces maintained for AI Meeting.
