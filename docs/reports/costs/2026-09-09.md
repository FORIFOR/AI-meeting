## Rate evidence and limitations

[Attendee's pricing](https://attendee.dev/pricing) lists $0.50 per bot-hour and five trial hours. The authenticated purchase dialog confirmed $0.50/$0.40/$0.35 per credit tiers; no purchase was executed. Billing states 1 credit equals one bot-hour. The local checkout of Attendee's `Bot.centicredits_consumed` rounds active heartbeat duration up per bot to 0.01 credit. Planning uses that rounding; the actual total uses deductions, not inferred durations.

[Vertex AI pricing](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/pricing) lists Gemini 2.5 Flash Live per million tokens: text input $0.50, audio/video/image input $3, text output $2, audio output $12. Those rates are not directly interchangeable with bot-hour credits.

The $2.27 display is consumed-credit value at the standard rate, including free credits. It is **not an invoice or actual cash payment**. Historic Gemini usage was not fully retained/reconciled; the Google Cloud billing console requires user reauthentication, and no billing-export datasets were returned for the project. AI, cloud infrastructure, other historical provider accounts, taxes, exchange fees and purchase discounts are not invented or treated as zero. A 150 JPY/USD default is explicitly hypothetical and editable, not a market quote.
